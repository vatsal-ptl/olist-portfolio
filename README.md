# Olist E-Commerce Portfolio

Analysis of Brazilian e-commerce data using SQL and Power BI.

## Tools
- MySQL 8.0
- Power BI

## SQL Analysis
10 business questions answered on the Olist dataset.
See: [olist_queries.sql](sql/olist_analysis.sql)

**Questions covered:**
- Revenue and orders by status
- Top 10 categories by revenue
- Customer distribution by state
- Top 10 sellers by revenue
- Month-over-month revenue change
- Customer retention 2017 → 2018

## Power BI Dashboard
![Olist Dashboard](powerbi/olist_dashboard.pdf)

**Key Findings:**
- Revenue grew 18x from late 2016 to early 2018
- November 2017 spike likely driven by Black Friday
- 97% order delivery rate
- Credit card dominates at 78% of payments
- Top category: Beauty & Health at 1.26M
- Sept-Oct 2018 data incomplete — not an actual decline
